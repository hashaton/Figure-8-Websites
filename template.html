Frequently asked questions

What's included in the $400?

A custom-designed website (up to 5 pages) with your business info, a contact form, and mobile-friendly styling — plus one round of revisions before launch.

What isn't included?

Domain registration and hosting are billed separately (usually $15–$30/year total) since those are paid to third-party providers, not to us. We'll walk you through setup.

Can you build more than 5 pages?

Our standard package covers up to 5 pages, which fits most small businesses. If you need more pages, online booking, or e-commerce, reach out and we can quote a custom project.

How long does it take?

Typically about a week from when we have your business info to a live site, depending on how quickly we get feedback on the preview.

Do I own the website?

Yes. Once it's paid for, the site is yours. Domain and hosting accounts are set up in your name.
